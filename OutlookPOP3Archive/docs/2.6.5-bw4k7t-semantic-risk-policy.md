BW4k7t stops treating an attempt to discover the undocumented purpose of PR_MESSAGE_FLAGS 0x00010000 as a prerequisite for progress. No authoritative semantics were established; a bit change is not proof of damage and not proof of safety. ## Prospective SEM-001-BW4k7t-Prospective-v1 classification

- Class A, CriticalSemanticOrIdentityMismatchBlocked: source/destination cardinality, exact POP3 store, immutable single Attempt/Intent, recipient set, sender, subject, text/HTML, transport headers, date, SHA-256 of attachment payload, attachment count/order, categories/follow-up, READ/HASATTACH mask, or Outlook restart readback differs. No waiver; STOP.
- Class B, expected or explained provider identity/size metadata changes: store-specific EntryID may change, MailItem.Size and PR_MESSAGE_SIZE may drift. Record exact values; size alone is neither proof of corruption nor automatic acceptance. A documented mfUnmodified mask 0x2 difference also does not authorize replay.
- Class C, unclassified PR_MESSAGE_FLAGS 0x00010000: if the only raw flag changes are the exact observed unknown 0x10000 and known 0x2 and ALL mandatory semantic witness booleans are independently true, classify as PotentialSemanticMatchUndocumentedProviderDifferenceReviewOnly. Missing evidence, unknown new raw bits, or any meaningful mismatch cannot be automatically accepted. Class C is eligible only for a FUTURE SEPARATE per-fixture operator risk decision; this implementation never creates or records a decision or authorizes Move.

The candidate is not a statement of full MAPI equivalence. The test matrix uses synthetic data, NOT proof of live recipient/attachment coverage. Historical BW4k7o read-only content match is narrower than production-level semantic proof, and cannot be retroactively upgraded.
