## Store identity

Microsoft Outlook enumeration uses olFolderSentMail=5, olFolderOutbox=4, olFolderInbox=6. The POP3 DeliveryStore is bound by unique AccountType=2/Account.DeliveryStore and exact local PST FilePath. Call Store.GetDefaultFolder on that store only; DO NOT use Namespace.GetDefaultFolder (may return different account's default store). Verify StoreID and EntryID. Never identify sent or outbox by localized names such as Отправленные or Исходящие.

References: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/api/outlook.oldefaultfolders and https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/api/Outlook.Store.GetDefaultFolder

Includes: exact Inbox and Sent Mail store folders; user-created mail-type folders and nested subfolders. Excludes: Outbox (distinct from Sent); Deleted, Drafts, Junk and other recognized system/nonmail folders. Exclusion is inherited by descendants. If POP3 Sent missing, report unresolved; do not pretend a similarly named user folder proves it. Sent in the *POP3* store does not prove all historical sent messages were saved there; other store destinations require separate read-only investigation.

## Bounded modes and completeness

Mode 1, default: structure and folder counts only. No item enumeration. Outputs FolderInventory entries with FolderPath, Role, ItemsCount, IncludedInFutureScope. This is the recommended first run.

Mode 2, -ScanYears: bounded read-only MailItem class/date/size metadata enumeration, ReceivedTime for Inbox/UserMail and SentOn for Sent; fallback date and missing dates tracked. Defaults MaxFolders=5000, MaxTotalItems=5000, MaxItemsPerFolder=5000. Partial output is never claimed complete. The 90-GB PST may require higher caps on a later approved inventory. Sum of MailItem.Size is NOT physical PST bytes.

Mode 3, -ScanYears -InspectAttachmentMetadata: optionally inspect Attachments.Count and at most MaxAttachmentsPerMail (50 default) Attachment.Size metadata per mail; never byte reads, SaveAsFile, attachment SHA claims. Excess attachment counts explicitly make evidence incomplete. This can be expensive.

Any unstable folder counts, source Outlook process restart, wrong store, duplicate folder EntryID, limits on folder topology or inaccessible required identity fails closed. All year archive filenames (e.g. info@stios.ru.Archive-2025.pst) are PLANS ONLY: no PST creation, rollover selection, capacity check or Move. Capacity policy remains separate, MaxArchivePstSize, SafetyMargin and rollover Archive-YYYY-02.pst and so forth.

## How to run on Windows PowerShell ISE 5.1 STA

Run from the extracted BW4k7p project root:

~~~powershell
.\Invoke-CI.ps1 -ExpectedBuild '2.6.3-PREVIEW1'

$scope = & '.\Core\Diagnostics\Invoke-OPA265BW4k7pPop3FolderScopeInventory.ps1'
$scope | Format-List Status, SourcePst, SourcePstLengthBytes, FolderCount, SentItemsResolvedInThisPop3Store, SentItemsDefaultFolderEntryId, OutboxDefaultFolderEntryId, EligibleFolderItemsObserved, ExcludedFolderItemsObserved, WriterPermit, ProductionReady
$scope.FolderInventory | Format-Table FolderPath, Role, ItemsCount, IncludedInFutureScope -AutoSize

# Bounded sample only, not guaranteed complete on Inbox with 26541 messages
$years = & '.\Core\Diagnostics\Invoke-OPA265BW4k7pPop3FolderScopeInventory.ps1' -ScanYears -MaxTotalItems 5000 -MaxItemsPerFolder 5000
$years | Format-List Status, YearItemsExamined, YearScanComplete, YearItemsDateUnavailable, SentItemsResolvedInThisPop3Store
$years.ProposedYearArchiveNames | Format-Table Year, ObservedMailItems, ProposedArchiveName, Coverage -AutoSize
$years.SentItemsYears | Format-Table Year, ObservedMailItems, Coverage -AutoSize

# Optional smaller attachment metadata sample
$attach = & '.\Core\Diagnostics\Invoke-OPA265BW4k7pPop3FolderScopeInventory.ps1' -ScanYears -InspectAttachmentMetadata -MaxTotalItems 1000 -MaxItemsPerFolder 1000
$attach | Format-List Status, AttachmentCountObserved, AttachmentSizeScanComplete, WriterPermit, ProductionReady
~~~

An explicitly higher MaxTotalItems and MaxItemsPerFolder may be used after the structure/small scans are reviewed; this can take significant time. No scan output is durable evidence for production, and no user content is written to disk.
