# 2.6.5-BW4e1 — -Review SwitchParameter collision hotfix (DEV, read-only)

## Root cause / correction

The BW4e diagnostic Test-OPA265BW4eFreshSyntheticReadOnly.ps1 defines
[Switch]$Review and later assigned the BW4d PSCustomObject readback to
$review. PowerShell variables are CASE-INSENSITIVE: Review and review
are one variable. Consequently the attempted assignment cast the
PSCustomObject to SwitchParameter and failed before the history replay
decision could be reported.

BW4e1 renames ALL uses of the readback local to $bw4dSnapshot and
preserves the explicit switch $Review. It adds 5 native Windows
PowerShell 5.1 regressions, including invoking the REAL BW4e diagnostic
wrapper against an isolated in-TestDrive BW4d mock result (no Outlook
COM, no real PST or historical journal touched), no-Move flags,
switch consent and AST-level no shadowed switch assignment check.
