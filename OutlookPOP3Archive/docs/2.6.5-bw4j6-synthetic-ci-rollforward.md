## Why isolated synthetic instead of real Outlook

Two distinct historical disposable Outlook POP3-to-PST MailItem.Move operations succeeded. One independently measured its SourceSize=8760 before Move and DestinationSize=8798 after Move, Subject/Sender/Body/ReceivedTime equal and +38 Size drift manually accepted under exact TestId. THIS DOES NOT prove that five other messages with HTML, Unicode, or attachments will also exhibit +38 Size delta or that all MAPI bytes persist.

BW4j6 models +38 as a *synthetic injected input*, not an Outlook expectation or general size waiver. CI exercises protocol invariants and compares actual synthetic decoded attachment byte SHA256, not live Outlook, MAPI properties or .pst physical durability.

## Phases

- BW4j4: five deterministic source JSON fixtures (PlainSmall, UnicodePlain, HtmlBody, Attachment1K, Attachment256K) with a write-once manifest and original byte hashes. Before each simulated atomic filesystem rename, write immutable per-TestId attempt claim with Flush(true) to `claims/TestId.json`. Duplicate attempts prohibited even if a crash left source in place.
- BW4j5: independently re-read manifest/claim/target, compare per-field UTF-8 digests and base64-decoded attachment bytes, record read-only reconciliation in a separate write-once receipt. Aggregate all five unique receipts; no claim journal rewrites.
- BW4j6: run one complete five-item series then four fault categories: crash immediately after claim, crash immediately after simulated move, duplicate source+destination presence, one-byte destination corruption. A missing/ambiguous source/target is quarantined. AfterMove permits reconciliation-only, never a second move.

1. UTF-8 BOM, PSScriptAnalyzer and Pester — expected 1968 tests, 80 containers.
2. Separate process `Core/Diagnostics/Invoke-OPA265BW4j6SyntheticMatrix.ps1 -RequireGitHubActions -SandboxParent $env:RUNNER_TEMP`, which hard-denies non-GitHub execution and uses a unique directory named `OPA265-BW4j6-SYNTH-<guid:N>`.

All writes are confined to runner TEMP sandbox. No network email, no Outlook COM, no PST, no original historical BW3a journal, no old BW4g claim or BW4i exact manual decision.
