## BW4k2 semantics

`Invoke-OPA265BW4k2IndependentReadOnly.ps1` requires `-Review` and
Windows PowerShell 5.1 STA. It checks the exact original Plan, all five source
witnesses, and Claim via pinned SHA-256 before and after TWO sequential
independent BW4j DestinationReview calls in the existing Outlook profile.
It binds each property of the immutable Claim to source and destination
MAPI identities and reports `MoveAttemptIndependentlyClaimed=True` only
after verifying Claim content and hash in this audit.

The script reports
`RealDisposableSourceZeroDestinationOneContentMatchSizeDriftUnaccepted`.
This status is **not** a size waiver, proof of byte equality, crash durability,
transaction completion, production readiness or permission to retry or
move the remaining four fixtures.
