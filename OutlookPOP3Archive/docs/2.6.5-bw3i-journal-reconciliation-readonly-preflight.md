### Legacy LIVE6 writer architecture reviewed (NOT INVOKED)

The separate LIVE6 -Execute implementation would require explicit
-ConfirmSyntheticJournalResolution, exact ExpectedJournalSha256, and
for cross-PST drift -ConfirmCrossPstSizeDifference with
-ExpectedArchiveSizeBytes 1444. Its writer uses a new durable
live6-resolution.claim before any journal append and an exclusive
FileStream lock with byte SHA256 check. A crash between claim
creation and append leaves a *permanent no-retry stop* pending manual
investigation. Do not treat this runbook as permission to execute it.

BW3i only documents/verifies these preconditions. Any subsequent
write design must assess the irreversible crash gap, run-specific
human consent, pre/post verification, and the size-record provenance.
Even with BW3i PASS, journal append requires a NEW independently
approved step and cannot be inferred from size consent.
