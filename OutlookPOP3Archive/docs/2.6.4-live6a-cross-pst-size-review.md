## Separate journal-only Execute protocol, not authorized by this runbook automatically
After an independently reviewed post-Move result with unchanged journal, it MAY be permitted to append the original one-shot synthetic Reconciled event:
- For identical sizes: original -Execute -ConfirmSyntheticJournalResolution -ExpectedJournalSha256 pinned-journal-sha.
- For different sizes: an ADDITIONAL -ConfirmCrossPstSizeDifference and -ExpectedArchiveSizeBytes exactly matching the separately inspected destination size are REQUIRED, in addition to the original two Execute gates.
- LIVE6a re-inspects Outlook and the original journal, then records both sizes and explicit approval in the durable single-use claim before an exclusive journal compare-and-append.
- If the Outlook-reported destination size changed even by one byte since -Review, Execute fails closed, creating NO new claim.
- No Worker, Move, PST edit, Inbox inspection, journal truncation, automatic retry, or production integration.

Never rerun -Execute after success or an indeterminate claim. Fail closed; preserve evidence.
